What proof do public energy programs ask for, and what do they show?

The Ledger rates 24 federal and New York State programs that fund municipal energy and infrastructure on three questions. Every rating links to the document and section it comes from.

R
Required verification. From financial reports only (0) to independent verification (4).
C
Public results. From nothing published (0) to open project-level data (4).
A
Attributability. From no baseline (0) to a comparison group or impact evaluation (3).

In version 0.1.0, 11 of 24 programs require no more than output reporting. Of the 5 that require measured or independently verified outcomes, none publish project-level outcome data.
