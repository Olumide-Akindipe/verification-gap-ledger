# Limitations: Verification Gap Ledger v0.1

*Plain-language note: what the Ledger cannot tell you. Read this before quoting any finding.*

1. **C measures publication, not collection.** Agencies may collect outcome data they do not publish. A C0 rating means no public results were found, not that none exist.
2. **v0.1 is a first sample, not the full universe.** It covers 24 programs (15 federal, 9 New York State), chosen to span agencies, funding vehicles, and asset classes. It is not a random or complete sample, so its distributions describe these programs only.
3. **Single coder.** Every v0.1 code was assigned by one coder and confirmed by the maintainer against its cited source. Inter-coder agreement is first measured in v0.5.
4. **Many codes rest on secondary official material.** Program pages often defer reporting rules to award terms or application packages not reviewed here. These codes carry low confidence; the maintainer confirmed each against the material cited, and v0.5 checks them against award terms.
5. **Requirements change.** Several programs were suspended, rescinded, terminated, or litigated in 2025 and 2026. Codes reflect sources accessed in October 2026.
6. **Ordinal scales are not interval scales.** The gap scores subtract ordinal codes for convenience. A gap of 2 is not twice a gap of 1.
7. **Program design, not project performance.** The Ledger rates what programs require and publish. It does not judge whether any project saved energy.
8. **Tax credits are coded at the credit-mechanism level.** Production-based and investment-based credits differ in what they measure; v0.1 codes the elective pay mechanism as a whole.
9. **Automated retrieval was not possible in the build environment.** Sources were read through a web reader and logged by hand with URL and access date, not by scripted download with checksums.
